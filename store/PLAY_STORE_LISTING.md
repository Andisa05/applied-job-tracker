# Google Play listing: Applied

**App name (30 max):** Applied: Job Hunt Tracker

**Short description (80 max):**
Track job applications, get follow-up reminders and learn CV & interview tips.

**Full description:**

Job hunting is hard enough without losing track of where you applied. Applied keeps your whole job search in one place, so you always know what to do next.

TRACK EVERY APPLICATION
• Save the job title, company, date, salary range, advert link, contact person and notes
• Mark each one as Applied, Interview, Offer, Rejected or No reply
• Search and filter your list in seconds

NEVER FORGET TO FOLLOW UP
• Set a follow-up date and Applied reminds you at 9am that day
• See overdue and upcoming follow-ups on your home screen
• Keep track of upcoming interviews

SEE YOUR PROGRESS
• Applications this week, interviews, offers and your response rate at a glance
• Stay motivated with a weekly target

TIPS THAT GET INTERVIEWS
• How to write a CV recruiters actually read
• Cover letters that stand out
• Where to find jobs, learnerships and graduate programmes in South Africa
• How to spot job scams
• Interview prep with the STAR method and common questions
• How and when to follow up

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account or sign-up needed
• Your applications stay on your phone
• Copy a backup any time

Applied is free and supported by ads. Made in South Africa.
