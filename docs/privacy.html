Applied: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Applied ("the app") is a free job application tracker made by Andisa Mtshali, an independent developer in Johannesburg, South Africa. This policy explains what information the app handles. It is written to meet the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) and Google Play's requirements.

Information you enter

The job applications, notes, dates, contacts and other details you type into Applied are stored only on your device. They are not sent to the developer or to any server. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, this information is deleted. The "Copy backup" feature copies your data to your clipboard so that you can save it wherever you choose.

Reminders

If you set a follow-up date, the app schedules a notification on your device. Notification permission is only used for these reminders, and you can turn it off at any time in your phone's settings.

Advertising

Applied is free because it shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device type and how you interact with ads. This is handled by Google under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice later in the app under More > Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings under Google > Ads (or Privacy > Ads).

What the developer does not do

  • The developer does not collect, sell or share the information you enter in the app.
  • The app does not require an account, and it does not access your contacts, photos, files or precise location.

Children

Applied is meant for job seekers and is not directed at children under 13.

Your rights

Because your app data stays on your device, you control it directly and can view, change or delete it at any time. For questions about this policy, or to exercise your rights under POPIA, contact the developer at andisap1@icloud.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
